Telnyx now offers mobile phone numbers in four Indian telecom districts, giving businesses a local presence in India with two-way voice calling over the Telnyx private network . Businesses can now establish a local presence, make and receive calls within India, and provision numbers through the Mission Control Portal without building or managing their own local telephony infrastructure.
