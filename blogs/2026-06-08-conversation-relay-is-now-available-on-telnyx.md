Telnyx Conversation Relay streams transcribed conversation text over WebSockets, so you can integrate voice calls with text-based AI workflows without handling raw audio. Conversation Relay sits between Telnyx telephony and your AI application. Telnyx manages the voice transport, real-time STT, and TTS, while your application handles the language model and business logic.
