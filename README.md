## APIs
- **Telnyx Voice API** — Programmable voice calling, conferencing, recording, transcription and TeXML.
- **Telnyx Messaging API** — SMS, MMS, RCS and number-pool messaging globally.
- **Telnyx Numbers API** — Search, order, port, and manage phone numbers worldwide.
- **Telnyx Verify API** — OTP/2FA verification via SMS, voice and flash call.
- **Telnyx Fax API** — Programmable T.38 and IP fax.
- **Telnyx Wireless API** — Wireless SIMs and private LTE networks for IoT.
- **Telnyx Networking API** — WireGuard, private networks and cross-connect provisioning.
- **Telnyx AI API** — AI assistants, streaming chat completions, speech, and embeddings.
- **Telnyx Billing & Reporting API** — Billing, payments, notifications and usage reporting.
